POS Multi Currency

Accept Point of Sale payments in any currency. Pick the allowed currencies per POS, convert amounts live on the payment screen, and keep the original foreign amount on the payment line, the receipt, and the backend order.
Key Features

Per-POS Setup

Enable multi currency per POS configuration and choose exactly which currencies the cashier can accept.

Live Conversion

The payment screen shows the exchange rate and the order total in the selected currency, using your accounting rates.

Full Traceability

The foreign amount and currency are shown on the payment line, printed on the receipt, and stored on the backend order.

How To Use

  • Activate the currencies you need and set their rates (Accounting > Currencies)
  • Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings > Payment: enable Multi Currency and pick the allowed currencies
  • On the payment screen, select a payment method, pick the currency, type the amount received and hit Update Amount
  • The payment line is converted to your company currency; the foreign amount stays visible everywhere
How It Works
A shop that keeps its books in USD takes a payment in Indonesian Rupiah, step by step.

1. Enable It Per POS

Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings > Payment: tick Multi Currency and pick the currencies your cashiers may accept. Rates come straight from Accounting > Currencies.

2. The Panel Tells The Cashier What To Ask For

Order total is $367.77. The customer wants to pay in Rupiah. Select IDR: the panel shows the rate (1 USD = Rp 17,962) and the total to collect (Rp 6,605,884.74). The customer hands over Rp 6,605,885, the cashier types it in and hits Update Amount — the payment line is converted back to $367.77 automatically. Remaining: $0.00.

3. Overpaid? Change In Both Currencies

If the customer hands over more, the payment line keeps the original foreign amount — Cash (2,555,555 Rp) = $216.64 — and the change shows in your company currency and, inside the panel, converted to the foreign currency so the cashier knows exactly how many Rupiah to hand back.

4. The Books Stay In Your Currency

The receipt prints both amounts — Cash (Rp 6,605,885) $367.77 — but your accounting never leaves USD. The foreign amount is stored as a witness of the physical cash received: check it on the backend order under the Payments tab, columns Amount in Currency and Paid Currency.
